## 3. What to do — by role

### Everyone (all pilot members)

1. Go to **Appraisal → Tasks**.
2. Complete **Monthly self-assessment** (use the **Month** selector, e.g. `2026-06`).
3. Complete every **360° peer review** listed for you (use the **Quarter** selector, e.g. `2026-Q2`).
4. Check **Appraisal → My feedback** for your anonymous 360 averages (visible after HR release for L2; L0/L1 see team aggregates in Directory/Insights).

**Legend**

| Colour | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Green | You also complete **quarterly performance self-assessment** |
| Orange | You also **give comments** (Appraisal → Comments) |
| Blue | You also **receive comments** from leaders above you |

## 5. Step-by-step: completing a task

1. Sign in with your email + `BoomEoDemo2026!`
2. Open **Appraisal** (default tab).
3. Set **Quarter** (360 & performance) and **Month** (monthly self) at the top.
4. Under **Tasks**, each card is one assignment:
   - **Monthly self-assessment** — private reflection.
   - **Executive assessment** — quarterly performance self (green roles).
   - **360° peer review** — scored, anonymous to the reviewee.
5. Click **Start** → answer all questions → **Submit**.
6. Use **Save draft** if you need to finish later.
7. Orange roles: also complete **Comments** for each blue person listed.

## 7. Privacy & anonymity

- **360 scores:** Reviewers are **never** shown to the reviewee — only averages.
- **Comments:** Recipients see text **without** reviewer names.
- **L2 self-view:** Your own 360 chart stays hidden until **HR/admin releases** that quarter.
- **Directory / Insights:** Leaders see **aggregates only**, not who said what.
